About VectorFlow

We're on a mission to automate the repetitive

Founded in 2023, VectorFlow helps modern teams focus on creative work by automating the tedious.

From frustration to innovation

Like many startups, we were spending hours manually moving data between Slack, Notion, and GitHub. We built VectorFlow to solve our own problem — and decided to share it with the world.

Today, thousands of teams use VectorFlow to save hundreds of hours on repetitive work, letting them focus on what really matters: building great products.
